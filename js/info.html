The internet, with the volume turned down.

Orbit is a social app built around genuine curiosity instead of performance. Follow ideas, meet people who notice the same little things, and make a place that feels like yours.

No shouting required.

Thoughtful posts and calm conversations are the point. The feed is designed for wandering, not winning.
